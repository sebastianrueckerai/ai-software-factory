## Observations

- The pattern we built (sandbox holds no key, a gateway injects credentials and enforces default-deny egress per task) became a product category in 2026: OpenShell, Agent Vault, iron-proxy, OneCLI, Claw Patrol, Cloudflare outbound Workers, Superserve Secrets, CubeSandbox egress gateway, Agyn. Most enforce through an HTTPS proxy variable plus MITM TLS; the serious ones pair it with kernel or network policy, as we do with Cilium.
- OpenShell's policy advisor (denials turned into narrow proposed rules, checked by a formal prover, approved by a human, hot-reloaded) is the most interesting idea against our static capability YAML.
- Hard budget holds before the call: Cycles implements the same reserve, commit, release pattern as our gateway, as an open protocol. Anthropic (session cents cap) and AWS (AgentCore gateway ceilings) shipped the same in August 2026.
- For our "gVisor is slow, microVMs need bare metal" weakness: Edera (paravirtualised Type-1, no nested virtualisation, RuntimeClass) and Kata on Cloud Hypervisor are the options; CubeSandbox and OpenSandbox give E2B-compatible self-hosted microVM pools on Kubernetes.
- Identity: the market consolidated (Astrix to Cisco, StrongDM to Delinea, SGNL to CrowdStrike, Natoma to Snowflake). Keycard and Aembit remain independent and closed; both bind short-lived credentials to agent, human and task, the same idea as our projected ServiceAccount token.
