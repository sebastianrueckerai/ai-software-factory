# Give a card more room: implementation plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** one control, "Give more room", gives a card a count of sessions' worth of time and money, ahead of time and at every ask about its resources. The human, the chat, the check-in, the factory agent and the factory's code all use it through one action, and a running session uses the room without a restart.

**Architecture:** a pure function in `factoryd` (`RoomFor`) turns a count into the raises of the session cap, the agent-time cap, the budget and the spend limit. The action `extend` takes the count and a money flag and replaces `extend`'s old arguments, `fund` and `raise_ceiling`. Act checks and applies it in one transaction. The console renders a readout from the same function, and the runner lengthens a running session when its card's time grows.

**Tech Stack:** Go (`factoryd`, the console's `html/template` with htmx, `agent-worker`), Temporal, Postgres, the console lab (`scripts/console-lab`).

**Spec:** [card-room-spec-2026-11-20.md](../card-room-spec-2026-11-20.md). Its sections bind every task. Section numbers below (§) refer to it.

**Where the work runs:** locally, in the worktree `~/repos/ai-software-factory-code/.worktrees/card-room` on branch `card-room`, cut from `origin/main` (`9754c6afb`). Paths are relative to that repository's `internal/` unless they start with `cmd/`, `scripts/`, `acceptance/` or `docs/`. Line numbers are from `9754c6afb`. `design/` paths are in this repository.

## Global Constraints

- One way only. When this branch lands, no `fund` or `raise_ceiling` action, no `agent_time_s`, `lines` or `eur` argument of a card's room, no `Resolution.Fund`, no `MaxExtendMinutes`, no `grant_session` or card `fund` chat tool and no line raise exist. Their tests go or change with them.
- `MaxRoomSessions = 5`. A count is a whole number from 1 to 5. The default count is 1.
- A session's worth: time = the card's agent time / `Attempts`, else `AnswerSlice` of the agent-time cap, never below `minSession` (5 minutes). Money = `EnforcedSpent` / `Attempts`, else the opening floor, never below the opening floor (§5.1).
- Room: `now = min(startsLeft, floor(timeLeft/T), floor(moneyLeft/P))`, at least 0, and each resource rises only by what it lacks for `now + n` (§5.2). A count of k is one call with n = k.
- Who decides what: the factory's code (rung 0) gives starts and time, never money. The check-in (rung 1) also gives money. The factory agent (rung 2) and a human (rung 3) also raise the spend limit.
- A human's grant does not count against the escalation pool. Money above the project's money left this month is refused for every decider (§5.5).
- Every bound is enforced in Go. No model sets a cap, a price or a figure on a face.
- Every text a human reads passes `writingguide.Lint`, calls a human a human, and never shows the words rung, case or pool. The rules of "Every card readable" hold: one label from the vocabulary, one effect line, fields only after the press, at most 4 answers on an asking face.
- Tests come first and derive their expectations by hand from the spec. No assertion is weakened to pass.
- Verify with `scripts/pg-test.sh go test -race -p 1 ./...` and `bash check static`. Postgres-backed tests need the throwaway Postgres that `scripts/pg-test.sh` starts.
- No ruling number and no ledger path in a tracked file or a commit message. Commit messages end with `Co-Authored-By: Claude Opus 5.5 <noreply@anthropic.com>`.
- Comments stay minimal: only what the code cannot say.

## Review Focus

1. **A card whose price reads as 0** (no session yet and no floor read, so P = 0). Expected: money does not limit `now`, no money is added, and nothing divides by zero. Pinned in Task 1.
2. **A card whose gates spent into the reserve** (`SessionLeft()` below 0). Expected: `now` is 0 and the money added covers the whole target from the negative figure, as `fundFor` does today. Pinned in Task 1.
3. **A count of 0, 6 or a fraction** from a hand-made form, a chat call or a rung's answer. Expected: refused with the range "a room counts 1 to 5 sessions' worth". Pinned in Task 4.
4. **Two humans press on the same card a second apart.** Expected: the second press meets changed figures and is refused with them, so nobody gives twice by accident. Pinned in Task 6.
5. **A project without its own month cap.** Expected: the month's money left is the factory's month headroom. Pinned in Task 3.

What the factory does now:

- One action, `extend` with `sessions` (1 to 5) and `money`, gives a card room. `fund` and `raise_ceiling` are gone, and so is the line raise.
- `RoomFor` works out one session's worth from the card's own sessions and raises each resource only by what it lacks. The money raises the spend limit first when it must.
- The opened card and every ask about a card's resources show "Give more room": five count buttons and a table of left, adds and then (C48, provisional until Sebastian picks). The readout is re-read on each change. The press carries a guard, which refuses a press whose room moved, except money that moved by at most 0.10 EUR.
- The chat has `give_room`. Its proposals carry the guard, and its confirm card names the minutes, the money and any raise of the spend limit.
- A human's grant leaves the escalation pool. A human's money is bounded by the project's money left this month, the figure the gateway admits calls against. On the day of the roll the code project's pool read 7 sessions, 6.8 hours and 25.00 EUR drawn under the new rule, against 55 sessions, 20.4 hours and 94.30 EUR under the old one.
- A running session takes a raise: its deadline, the supervisor's and the gate watch's bounds move with it, within a headroom of 5 sessions' worth, and the agent is told. A raise during a factoryd restart survives the reattach.
- Every give takes the card's lock, so two presses at once give once, and a give is weighed as bought nothing only after a session ended since it.

Each task passed a task review and its fix rounds, then a whole-branch review on Opus (the Fable seat hit its usage limit), one fix wave and two scoped re-reviews.
