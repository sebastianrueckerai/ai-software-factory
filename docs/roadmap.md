# Roadmap: the future topics of the software factory

This file is the factory's memory for future work. Every idea, wish, deferred item and known weakness that is not built lives here, in one place, where Sebastian sets priorities. One file hangs off it: [roadmap-risks-and-rejections.md](roadmap-risks-and-rejections.md) holds the accepted risks to revisit and the ideas rejected earlier. The board's own backlog, once a file of its own, lives here since 2026-10-28 as items 318 to 320.

It is not a record of rulings. Items get fuzzier the further out they are, and deleting a stale item is fine. A remark in a discussion is not a final decision. Where a ledger ruling decides something, the item cites it. When an item is built, its record goes to the stage paragraphs of the [build plan](plans/2026-09-24-build-plan.md) and the item leaves this file. The record of what landed between 2026-10-16 and 2026-10-25 is the plan's last section.

## Three guiding ideas

**Primitives, not a process.** The factory ships no fixed development process. It ships small primitives, and a user composes them into the process they want. Each primitive is a plain YAML block with a schema, in the spirit of Kubernetes objects but without Kubernetes vocabulary (ruling 184). `factoryd config check` refuses a composition that cannot work: a cycle, a lane that starts tasks with no class that may code, a queue on a stage that does not exist. Every item below is meant to be built as such a primitive.

**Fewer manual steps than the competition, with enough security.** The goal is software built and improved without human input. The factory aims for fewer manual steps and gates than other products while keeping the security it needs. Today the factory's code project runs at full auto, where the gate bot approves what the gate decided, and the high risk class merges on the audit's pass. Items such as the answer chain, blocked with a next action, mutation testing and the intervention rate give the evidence for the next step. Source: [goal.md](goal.md).

**Limited scope, full context, simple prompts.** Each step an AI takes has a limited scope. It gets all the context it needs, including the history of the steps before it, all the tools it needs, tools to gather more, and a clear view of the goal. It checks its own work and corrects itself, so its prompt stays simple. A weak spot gets its fix in code, in a tool's shape, in a check or in the configuration. A prompt rule is the last resort, since such rules are brittle and accumulate. A step that is too large is made smaller by construction, not by asking. Stage 16's four principles ([architecture.md](architecture.md) 5.7) are this idea's first form: no rule in a prompt that a tool or a deterministic step can carry, every console read is a tool, self-correction over prevention, and a small default context with depth fetched by tools. Every finding below names its fix in this way. Source: Sebastian, 2026-10-12.

## How to read this file

The sections are subjects, not a priority order. The order of 2026-10-14 is spent: its fixes, security and performance items are built. Inside a section, three bands sort the items by how far their plans are:

- **Ready to build.** The shape is decided, and a plan, a design or a ruling says what to build. A session can start on it.
- **Needs a decision, a design round or a spike.** The want is clear, the shape is not. Sebastian's decision, a design round or a measurement comes first.
- **Ideas, and what waits on a need.** Vague, far off, or deferred until the factory has the need.

Each item says what and why, where it comes from, where it stands, a size where a source gives one, and a `Priority` field that Sebastian fills in. Item numbers never change, since commits and cards cite them. A push whose roadmap repeats a number is refused (.githooks/pre-push, active through `git config core.hooksPath .githooks`). The later item takes the next free number and names its old one. An item built in part keeps only its open part here. An item that absorbed smaller ones names their numbers. The chat's `write_doc` tool refuses an edit of this file that repeats an item number or drops an item's Sources or Where-it-stands line. The next free number is 330.

Sources. Plan: the [build plan](plans/2026-09-24-build-plan.md). Its lists of 2026-09-29 (the harness list, the UX list, the market list, the second and third rounds) live here under their old numbers, which git history keeps. Arch: [architecture.md](architecture.md). Target: [target-architecture.md](target-architecture.md). Synthesis: the [market research synthesis](market-research/SYNTHESIS.md). Goal: [goal.md](goal.md). UX conversation: [the conversation on the human's side of the factory](ai-software-factory-ux-conversation.md). Evidence: [evidence.md](evidence.md), the claims page's source. Threat model: [threat-model.md](threat-model.md). The pitch: launch/revision/pitch.md, private and gitignored.
