# Roadmap: the future topics of the software factory

This file is the factory's memory for future work. Every idea, wish, deferred item and known weakness that is not built lives here, in one place, where Sebastian sets priorities. Two files hang off it: [roadmap-risks-and-rejections.md](roadmap-risks-and-rejections.md) holds the accepted risks to revisit and the ideas rejected earlier, and [board/roadmap.md](board/roadmap.md) holds the board's own backlog.

It is not a record of rulings. Items get fuzzier the further out they are, and deleting a stale item is fine. A remark in a discussion is not a final decision. Where a ledger ruling decides something, the item cites it. When an item is built, its record goes to the stage paragraphs of the [build plan](plans/2026-09-24-build-plan.md) and the item leaves this file.

## Three guiding ideas

**Primitives, not a process.** The factory ships no fixed development process. It ships small primitives, and a user composes them into the process they want. Each primitive is a plain YAML block with a schema, in the spirit of Kubernetes objects but without Kubernetes vocabulary (ruling 184). `factoryd config check` refuses a composition that cannot work: a cycle, a lane that starts tasks with no class that may code, a queue on a stage that does not exist. Every item below is meant to be built as such a primitive.

**Fewer manual steps than the competition, with enough security.** The goal is software built and improved without human input. The factory aims for fewer manual steps and gates than other products while keeping the security it needs. Today the factory's own repositories stay on the `human` merge rule, and every `auto` merge rests on the review's approve. Items such as the answer chain, blocked with a next action, mutation testing and the intervention rate give the evidence for taking the next step. Source: [goal.md](goal.md).

**Limited scope, full context, simple prompts.** Each step an AI takes has a limited scope. It gets all the context it needs, including the history of the steps before it, all the tools it needs, tools to gather more, and a clear view of the goal. It checks its own work and corrects itself, so its prompt stays simple. A weak spot gets its fix in code, in a tool's shape, in a check or in the configuration. A prompt rule is the last resort, since such rules are brittle and accumulate. A step that is too large is made smaller by construction, not by asking. Stage 16's four principles ([architecture.md](architecture.md) 5.7) are this idea's first form: no rule in a prompt that a tool or a deterministic step can carry, every console read is a tool, self-correction over prevention, and a small default context with depth fetched by tools. Every finding below names its fix in this way. Source: Sebastian, 2026-10-12.
