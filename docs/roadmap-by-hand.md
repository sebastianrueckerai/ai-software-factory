# Roadmap: the work done by hand

This file hangs off the [roadmap](roadmap.md) and uses its item format, its numbers and its key to the sources. It holds the items the factory cannot carry, whatever is decided: no card in the factory's own code project can do the work. A person does it, on the live system and its keys, in the infra repository, on another cloud or machine, or with an outsider. The infra repository has no agents (ruling 179, `agents: none`), so every change there is by hand too. Nothing here becomes a card.

The numbers are one series with the roadmap: a new item takes the next free number from the roadmap's header, and the pre-push hook refuses a repeat across the three files. An item whose carrier changes moves between the files under its own number. An item with a part the factory can carry gives that part a number of its own in the roadmap and names it. When an item is done, its record goes to the [build plan](plans/2026-09-24-build-plan.md) or the ledger and the item leaves.

The sections sort the items by what carries them.
