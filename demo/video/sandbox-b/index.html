Every card on the board runs in a sandbox of its own. Before the agent starts, a probe proves it cannot reach the internet, the host or the cloud metadata. Every model call goes through the gateway, held against the card's budget first. The agent never holds a push token or a model key. At the cap, the factory stops and asks you: fund it or end it. When the agent is done, the factory pushes once with a fresh token, revokes it and deletes the pod.
