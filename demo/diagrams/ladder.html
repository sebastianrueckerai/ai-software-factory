The escalation ladder

1

Self-correction

The agent runs the tests, reads the failure and corrects its own work, inside its session.

2

The stronger model

Code moves the card to the strong rung: at the cheap cap, when a session is stuck, or after returns from the gates.

cheap → strong

3

The check-in

A detector trips, a session ends without a push, or a gate returns findings: a strong model with read tools looks.

continue · message · handover · human

4

A fresh session

Starts from the check-in's handover note. The earlier transcripts lie in its workspace to search.

handover

5

The human

Gets a prepared question: what happened, what was tried, what the check-in found, the options.

answer · fund · grant · close
