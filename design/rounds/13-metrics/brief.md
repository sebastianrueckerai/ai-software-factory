# Round 13 — the metrics page

Roadmap 147, from the [metrics concept](../../../docs/metrics-visualization-concept-2026-10-19.md). Sebastian picked the info hierarchy in chat on 2026-10-19: instrument rows (value · move · spark under small-cap sections), with the strip's shared time axis carried as one direction of its own. This round turns that into a page design: six directions in both themes, two critics, Sebastian's pick, then the build.

## What the round designs

1. The metrics page of a project: how it ships, how steady the work is, what it costs, how much ran without a human, and what the factory itself did — over a window, against the window before.
2. The figure row: the value, the move against the previous window, and the weeks as a spark. How these three read together is the round's core question.
3. The window control (7 / 28 / 90 days) and where it sits.
4. How the deltas carry good against bad when up is good for throughput and bad for cost.

## What is fixed

- The shell: C23's top bar, with METRICS as the open page's word in the figures group. The entry point (a bar word against a link from the status strip) is a separate small decision, not drawn here.
- C24's readout grammar as the base: small-caps section labels, monospaced tabular figures, rows on the page ground between hairline rules, no card fills.
- The content deck: every direction shows exactly its words and numbers, nothing invented.
- The palette: the console's tokens, dark and light. Cyan stays reserved; what ink the sparks and bars get is a question, not a decision.
- The hierarchy: sectioned figure rows (value · move · spark), except direction 03, which exists to test the strip against the rows.

## The questions the candidates answer

- Do the sections read in weight order at a glance: delivery, stability, cost, autonomy, the factory itself?
- How does the spark sit against the value: inline after it, in its own right column, under the section's line?
- How do the deltas say good and bad without shouting: colour, sign alone, an arrow, muted strength?
- Does one section ever earn a wider chart (the headline direction) or does the row carry everything?
- Does the strip (03) beat the rows as the main view, or stay a second view?

## Limits

Six directions, both themes, one contact sheet per theme. Critics: gemini and glm (design/models.md). At most two loops. Spending cap $15; the expected cost is cents. One session. The director's recommendation is provisional; Sebastian's pick overrides it and becomes the C-entry.
