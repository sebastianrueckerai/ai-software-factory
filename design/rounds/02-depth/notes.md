# Round 02: depth on the carried ten, three hybrids

Per carried direction on `google/nano-banana-2.1`: the drawer open on the running card #601 (Chat tab), the phone at 9:16, and the idle moment. Plus three hybrids of the busy screen. 33 images, about $5, 12 minutes. Sheets: `contact-sheet-drawer.jpg`, `contact-sheet-phone.jpg`, `contact-sheet-idle.jpg`, `contact-sheet-hybrids.jpg`.

## What the images settle

- **The drawer docked right works in every direction.** The chat stays free on the left in 02, 03, 06, 09, 13 and 16; 01's mockup broke into three panels and is not a reference for the drawer.
- **The phone as one column, chat first, holds.** 02, 03, 04, 06, 09, 10, 13 and 16 show the order the brief asks for: the pulse, the status row, the chat with its composer, Needs you in full with full-width buttons, Working, then collapsed Verifying, Queued and Done. 01 and 11 rendered three tiny desktop screens instead: no reference, the builders follow ia.md.
- **Idle looks designed when the empty columns say "empty" quietly and the chat takes the width.** 04 and 06 are the best idle screens: the over-cap spend in red, the pulse subdued, nothing broken. 16 puts the over-cap into the chat dock's banner, which fits its idea.
- **Hybrid 06+07 (mission control with the trading floor's density) is stronger than 06 alone**: the amber tray, the dense monospace rows, the chat as a console on the right. It is the reference busy screen for the Mission control prototype. 01+10 and 09+16 add nothing over their parents.

## For the builders

The builders got these images in their fix loop as targets for the drawer, the phone and the idle state. Where a mockup contradicts ia.md (01's drawer, 11's phone), ia.md wins.
