DESIGN DIRECTION 4 — FIGURES. The money leads. Under the heading, before any
table, each project's envelope becomes a row of large tabular figures: four
stat columns per project block — "task block €10.00 /mo", "this month
€0.43", "open 1", "standing none" — the figures themselves at ~28 px
monospace, the labels small caps and muted beneath or beside them. These
figure rows are the page's visual anchor: aligned across projects into
shared vertical axes, so "this month" of one project lines up with "this
month" of the next. Below each figure row, the detail table is deliberately
quiet and small — hairline rules, muted small-caps headers, monospace
values — clearly secondary to the figures. The scheduled jobs of infra are
introduced by their own small figure ("nightly · 2 jobs") before their
table. The "gone" project shows only its dim sentence under its small-caps
name, no figures. No boxes around the figure groups; alignment and scale do
all the work. The impression: the page answers "what is this costing and
what is running" in the first 120 pixels, detail available but receded.
