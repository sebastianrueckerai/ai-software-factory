DESIGN DIRECTION 3 — SHEET. The page is a technical datasheet, one entry per
resource, key-value rows with dotted leaders. No tables. Each resource
becomes a compact block: first line the resource's name and its task ref in
monospace ("server 71 · #606"), slightly heavier; under it three to five
leader rows like "type ······ cx23", "ipv4 ······ 203.0.113.24", "lease
ends ······ in 2 h", "rate ······ 0.0072 EUR/h", "cost so far ······ 0.01
EUR", "state ······ live", the label in muted small caps, the value right-
aligned in monospace at a shared right edge, the dotted leader filling the
space between. A hairline rule separates blocks. Each project section starts
with its small-caps name and its envelope line set as one full-width leader
row of its own ("task block ······ €10.00 a month"). The purpose sentence
("acceptance run against a fresh Postgres") is a muted quoted line under the
block's title. Acts sit as quiet words at the block's foot. Two columns of
blocks side by side would be wrong: one column, ~720 px wide, centered-left,
like a printed spec sheet pinned to the screen.
