DESIGN DIRECTION 5 — RAIL. A sticky index rail on the left. The content area
keeps a left column, ~180 px wide, holding the page's own navigation: the
four project names in small caps, one per line, each with its live count as
a small monospace figure after it ("AI-SOFTWARE-FACTORY-CODE 1", "INFRA 2",
"DEMO-APP 1", "GONE —"), the current-scroll section's entry cyan with a
short left tick. The rail is separated from the content by one vertical
hairline. The content column holds the same sections as before — envelope
line, then table — but never needs a "back to top": the rail is the map.
The rail's top aligns with the page heading; the heading itself ("Resources"
with its intro) spans rail plus content. Tables keep hairline rules, no
fills; states carry the 8 px lamp squares. The impression: a long operations
page made navigable, like a man page or a spec with a contents column — but
quiet, no active backgrounds, the cyan tick the only moving color.
