DESIGN DIRECTION 6 — CARDS (the control, today's style evolved). Each project
is a rounded panel of the panel background color, 1 px hairline border, ~12
px corner radius, generous inner padding (~20 px). The panel's head row:
small-caps project name left, envelope figures right in monospace, hairline
under the head, then the table inside the card, its own hairline rules but
the card's border doing the grouping. Panels stack vertically with ~20 px
gaps, full content width. Tables keep monospace figures and quiet headers.
States are small words, not lamps. Acts are quiet words in the last column.
This is the most conventional enterprise reading of the page — included as
the baseline the others must beat: clean, boxed, a little heavier, air
trapped inside borders.
