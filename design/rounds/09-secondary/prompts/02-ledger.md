DESIGN DIRECTION 2 — LEDGER. The page is one continuous accounting table,
like a page of a bound ledger. Not one table per project: a single ruled
table spanning the full content width, where each project begins with a
small-caps divider row (project name left, envelope figures right, a heavier
1 px rule above it) and its rows follow. Every column keeps its exact
alignment down the whole page, so the eye can scan "EUR/h" or "State"
straight from top to bottom. Row height generous, ~40 px, single hairline
between rows, no fills, no zebra. The header row of column names appears
once, at the top, pinned under the page heading, quiet small caps. The jobs
section and the demo-app section are divider rows of the same continuous
table (their differing columns merge gracefully: empty cells stay empty,
never invented). The "gone" line is a dim italic row inside the table. Acts
are quiet words in the last column. Totals feel engraved rather than
highlighted: the envelope figures in the divider rows are the only bold text
on the page. The impression: one long ruled sheet, serious, financial.
