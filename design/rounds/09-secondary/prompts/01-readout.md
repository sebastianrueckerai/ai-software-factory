DESIGN DIRECTION 1 — READOUT. The page is one flat instrument readout, the
direct heir of the console's notification page. No panels, no boxes, no card
fills: content sits directly on the page background, separated only by
hairline rules. Each section is: a small-caps project label in letter-spaced
muted type, the envelope line directly under it in small monospace figures,
then the table. Tables are ruled with hairlines only — no zebra, no row
fills; the header row is quiet small caps on the hairline; column figures
are monospace and right-aligned where they are numbers. A tiny 8 px square
lamp before each state value: filled cyan for "live", hollow faint for
"deleted", faint dot for "ok". Section gaps do the work: about 48 px of air
between sections, a full-width hairline above each section label. Acts are
quiet text links; "Promote" and "Release" are same-size words, "Release" not
red. The whole page breathes horizontally: one 56-64 px left margin aligned
with the bar's own margin, table stretching to the right margin. Nothing
shouts; the numbers are the loudest thing on the page.
