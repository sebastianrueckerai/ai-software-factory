# Round 09 — the secondary pages

The condensed form. One system for the console's reference pages, decided by
this round and applied to every page that stays.

## What the round designs

The pages behind the top bar after the cut, and the cut itself:

1. **Board** goes from the bar: the home word opens the board.
2. **New task** stays as the bar's one action (C23's bordered word), not a
   destination. The chat is the intended path; the form is the no-chat path.
3. **Projects** goes as a cross-project page. Its per-project content becomes
   **Settings**, the open project's wiring (the nav select follows the page it
   is on). New project is a quiet link at Settings' foot; the filing flow and
   its status panel stay as they are.
4. **Alerts** goes: an alert files a card, the verdict buttons already live on
   the board's escalation card (architecture 5.6), and the notification
   channel carries the ping. The verdict routes stay.
5. **Costs, Resources, Policy** stay, redesigned. **Notifications** belongs to
   another session and is fixed as built (C19).
6. The bar carries the decided C23 design with the new destinations in two
   groups: the project's figures (**Costs, Resources**) and how the factory
   runs (**Settings, Policy, Notifications**). New task sits between the
   select and the groups as the one bordered action.

The system's question, which the candidates answer: **what one structure
carries a reference page** — its figures, its tables, its read-only facts,
its rare actions — so all four pages read as one design.

## What is fixed

- The shell: the C23 top bar (44 px, hairline rules, small-capitals words,
  the open page's word cyan with a short underline, theme toggle, user, Log
  out), the console's theme tokens and type (13 px sans UI, mono figures),
  the contrast floor (4.5:1 text), the phone rule (390, 44 px targets), the
  content width (80 rem), `h2` pages under the shell.
- The content: every control and data point of
  [inventory.md](../../inventory.md) stays reachable. The Alerts page's
  unique rows (raised, resolved, verdict, supervisor action) go with the
  page; the verdict stays on the board, the diagnosis already shows on the
  card. The Projects page's settings move to Settings; the envelope shows on
  Resources only, linked from Settings.
- The notifications page as built (C19): its readout language is a candidate
  system, not a constraint.

## The questions the candidates answer

1. Does the system give each page one obvious reading order — figures first,
   then the detail — without a dashboard of cards?
2. Do read-only facts state themselves as facts (value, source, link) without
   form chrome, and do the rare actions stay findable?
3. Does the system scale from Costs' two tables to Settings' mixed facts and
   acts, and down to the phone as stacked rows?
4. Does it rhyme with the console's instrument character (the strip, the
   thread table, the notifications readout) rather than introduce a second
   design language?

## The limits

- 8 directions as images (the Resources page, busy scenario, 1440, dark and
  light), nano-banana-2.1, $0.15 an image tracked as the actual rate.
- 2 carried into one fix loop (both themes), one compare pass with both
  critics.
- Generation cap $8. The pick is built in the code worktree; the lab's check
  must be green before and after; at most 2 fix loops on the built pages.
- The director's pixel pass runs through a vision model with a checklist
  prompt this session (image reads return URLs, not pixels); notes.md
  records every claim so checked.
