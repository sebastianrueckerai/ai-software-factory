The part names used in this description (title, why line, place, ask block, effect line, note field, summary, folds, ghost card, and card numbers in brackets such as (5)) only describe the parts. They never appear as text on the screen, unless the direction names a label to show. Draw no measurements, no pixel sizes and no annotations. Buttons show their label alone, without brackets. Legible UI text with exact spelling. Every word above appears once, where it is placed. Nothing invented, no lorem ipsum, no extra cards, buttons or labels. Normal states whisper, exceptional states speak: the Needs you cards and their amber answer buttons are the loudest things on the screen, queued and in progress cards are quiet, done cards are the quietest.
