Light theme, the warm cream day version: page #f1ece0, panels #f7f3ea, cards #fffdf8, hairlines #d9d4c6, text dark navy #0f1d33, muted #46536a, faint #5b6678, cyan #0a6a80, amber #f0a62a filling buttons with navy text, amber text #8a4f00, the Needs you fill #fff3d6, done green #1c7448, failed red #a14b43.

Two phone screens side by side on a plain neutral background with an even gap, each a flat mobile browser viewport of a 390 px wide phone, no device frame, no notch, no status bar, drawn at high resolution so the text is crisp. The part names used in this description (title, why line, place, ask block, effect line, note field, summary, folds, ghost card, and card numbers in brackets such as (5)) only describe the parts. They never appear as text on the screen, unless the direction names a label to show. Draw no measurements, no pixel sizes and no annotations. Buttons show their label alone, without brackets. Legible UI text with exact spelling. Every word above appears once, where it is placed. Nothing invented, no lorem ipsum, no extra cards, buttons or labels.
