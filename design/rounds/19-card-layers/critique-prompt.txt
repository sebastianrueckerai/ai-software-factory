THE PART UNDER REVIEW. The cards on the board and the opened card. A human who dropped in cold must understand each card: what it is, why it exists, where it stands. The cards show layers. Layer 3, the place: the plan's name, "card 1 of 3", what it waits for, the state and time. For a card that asks, layer 3 is the suggested answer as one button with who suggests it, and a count of the other answers. The opened card adds, in order: the ask block (each answer with its effect line, a note field that opens only after its answer is pressed, one Send), a summary, and five closed folds ("The full brief", "The plan", "The card's life", "What each session got", "Other actions").
