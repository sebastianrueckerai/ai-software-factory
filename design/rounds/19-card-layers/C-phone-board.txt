UI design mockup of a screen of an AI software factory console, in its existing "Mission control" design: thin-bordered instrument panels with small corner marks, cyan as the system colour, amber as the one signal that needs a human, monospace for refs, times and figures, a system sans for text, sizes 11 to 18 px, weights 400 and 600 only, everything exact and calm.

Light theme, the warm cream day version: page #f1ece0, panels #f7f3ea, cards #fffdf8, hairlines #d9d4c6, text dark navy #0f1d33, muted #46536a, faint #5b6678, cyan #0a6a80, amber #f0a62a filling buttons with navy text, amber text #8a4f00, the Needs you fill #fff3d6, done green #1c7448, failed red #a14b43.

Three phone screens side by side on a plain neutral background with even gaps, each a flat mobile browser viewport of a 390 px wide phone, no device frame, no notch, no status bar, drawn at high resolution so the text is crisp. No label such as "Why:", "Place:" or "Plan:" stands before any line.

The part names used in this description (title, why line, place, ask block, effect line, note field, summary, folds, ghost card, and card numbers in brackets such as (5)) only describe the parts. They never appear as text on the screen, unless the direction names a label to show. Draw no measurements, no pixel sizes and no annotations. Buttons show their label alone, without brackets. Legible UI text with exact spelling. Every word above appears once, where it is placed. Nothing invented, no lorem ipsum, no extra cards, buttons or labels. Normal states whisper, exceptional states speak: the Needs you cards and their amber answer buttons are the loudest things on the screen, queued and in progress cards are quiet, done cards are the quietest.
