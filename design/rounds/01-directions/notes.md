# Round 01: 16 directions

One busy screen per direction, dark on `google/nano-banana-2.1` and on `flux-3/text-to-image` (the second take), light on nano-banana. 48 images, about $5.60, 25 minutes. Contact sheets: `contact-sheet-dark.jpg`, `contact-sheet-light.jpg`, `contact-sheet-flux.jpg`. Critiques: `critique/gemini-dark.json`, `critique/gemini-light.json`. The flux takes are plainer in every direction and were not judged further.

## Director's view

Strong, carried forward:

- **06 Mission control.** The coolest screen of the set and still clear: the Needs you tray lit amber across the top, the board below, the chat as the console at the bottom. The one most likely to get "wow" from a visitor. Risk: the chat at the bottom is a dock, not the main region; the prototype must make the chat large.
- **09 Inbox first.** The clearest answer to "what needs me": an inbox on the left, the chat in the centre, the board folded to a strip on top. Risk: the board loses its place; the strip must expand into the real columns.
- **01 Linear calm.** The safe professional baseline. Chat left, board right, one blue. Nothing wrong with it; also nothing memorable. Carried as the control.
- **02 Geist contrast.** Pure black or white, rules not boxes, one amber for Needs you. Sharp. Risk: the board, as plain text rows, may read as the main thing.
- **16 Messenger dock.** The board on top, the chat as a full-width messenger at the bottom. The most "chat first" layout and the most friendly for a visitor. Light is its better theme.
- **04 Swiss grid.** The status figures as big numerals and the pulse as a headline, black, white and one red. Distinctive, typographic, the most "design studio". Risk: D7 of the portfolio rejected numerals as ornament; here they are the real figures, but the prototype must keep them modest.
- **10 Command palette.** The chat as a Raycast-like command bar in the top centre, the reply as a floating sheet, the board beneath. A genuinely different take on "chat as the main affordance". Risk: the sheet covers the board.
- **11 Terminal brutalist.** Amber phosphor and box-drawing, the paper-tape light. The wild pick: the critic calls it a cult statement, and Sebastian carried Terminal Noir in the portfolio round. The prototype must stay usable: real buttons, real text sizes.
- **13 Factory floor.** Yellow work orders for Needs you, the andon lamps, the chat as the radio at the bottom. Thematic and readable. Risk: gimmick; keep the lamps small.
- **03 Soft SaaS.** Warm, rounded, friendly; the light version is its real form, the dark one is muddy. The non-technical end of the range.

Dropped:

- **05 Broadsheet.** The board dominates and the headline eats the screen. The newspaper idea does not fit a tool.
- **07 Trading floor.** Dense and cool but too much for a visitor; its ticker and row density go into Mission control as a hybrid.
- **08 Grafana dense.** The board dominates; its KPI sparkline is a nice touch for the status figures and goes into Linear calm as an option.
- **12 Blueprint.** A wireframe, illegible.
- **14 Glass depth.** The layers read as a mess in both themes.
- **15 Riso.** A poster; blurry text. Fun on the portfolio, wrong on a tool.
