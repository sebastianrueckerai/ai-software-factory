DESIGN DIRECTION "lamp": one bar in the console's lamp language. The links in small capitals, 12 px: BOARD, NOTIFICATIONS, NEW TASK, PROJECTS, RESOURCES, COSTS, POLICY, ALERTS, grouped only by wider gaps: work, then reference pages, then governance. The current page carries a small cyan square lamp before its word; the others none. Notifications carries a small amber lamp while an ask is open. When the bar narrows the gaps close before anything folds.
