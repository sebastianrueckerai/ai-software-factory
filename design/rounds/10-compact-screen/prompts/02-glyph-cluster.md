DESIGN DIRECTION 2 — GLYPH CLUSTER. The chat's window controls become one
quiet cluster of icon glyphs at the right end of the chat's head, in the
grammar of a code editor's panel controls: a diagonal-arrow expand glyph
(chat across the whole screen), a people/thread glyph (the thread list),
each 16 px, muted until hover, each with a tooltip ("Chat across the whole
screen — the board hides", "Your threads"). When the chat IS full screen
the expand glyph swaps to its collapse twin (arrows inward) and the cluster
sits alone at the screen's top right; a faint "board" ghost of the board's
first column edge shows at the left as a promise of return. The border
between board and chat is a plain hairline that grows a three-dot grip on
hover; the chat never narrows below 24 rem with the thread list unfolded —
the threads table gets the width it needs and the conversation column
yields. The composer has no button at all: it auto-grows with the message
to about a third of the screen, then scrolls inside. The open status panel
compresses to two blocks: the spend gauges at the left; at the right a
single readout block where the dependencies are five name-plus-lamp chips
in a row (all green), one chip's tooltip visible reading
"up · under a minute ago", and above them one mono line
"model gateway up · GitHub sf/ai-software-factory · configuration 4f2c9e1".
A down dependency turns red and expands to a full loud row. Tooltips on
every glyph; nothing unlabelled.
