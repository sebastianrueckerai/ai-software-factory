DESIGN DIRECTION 5 — POPOVER. The status detail stops being a full-width
band and becomes a compact popover, about 34 rem wide, docked at the
strip's right end above it, hairline-ruled with the page's shadow grammar:
inside, top, the two spend gauges (today, this month) side by side; under
them one mono readout line "model gateway up · 2 runs waiting on the
provider · GitHub sf/ai-software-factory · configuration 4f2c9e1"; under
that the dependencies as five lamp chips in a wrap row — 7 px green dot
plus service name in small mono, each chip's tooltip reading
"up · under a minute ago"; a down service turns its chip red with its age
inline. The strip itself is unchanged: one thin row of cells with its
dependencies lamp. The chat: the head keeps its title and one quiet
expand-glyph button at the far right (tooltip "Chat across the whole
screen — the board hides"); full screen swaps the glyph to its inward twin
and keeps it in the same place; the border between board and chat is a
hairline with a hover grip. The chat's floor is 24 rem, the unfolded
threads table never squeezed. The composer auto-grows to a third of the
screen then scrolls inside, no button. The popover's compactness is the
point: the whole status detail fits a third of the screen's width, so the
board behind it stays readable with the detail open.
