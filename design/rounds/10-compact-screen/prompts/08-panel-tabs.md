DESIGN DIRECTION 8 — PANEL TABS. The chat becomes a tabbed panel, an
editor's side panel: a small tab strip under its head — "CHAT" (the
conversation) and "THREADS ‹N›" (the thread list) — so the unfolded
threads never squeeze the conversation: they are a tab, they get the
panel's whole width, and the panel's floor is 24 rem either way. At the
tab strip's right end the window controls: an expand glyph (chat across
the whole screen; tooltip says so) that swaps to its inward twin when
full, and the same grammar holds the board's wide-view toggle if it ever
moves here. The border between board and chat is a hairline with a hover
grip, tooltip "Drag to resize — double-click to reset". The composer
auto-grows to a third of the screen, then scrolls inside. The open status
panel: the spend gauges at the left; at the right one readout line
("model gateway up · 2 runs waiting on the provider · GitHub
sf/ai-software-factory · configuration 4f2c9e1") and the dependencies as
five lamp chips with names, ages on hover, a down service red and loud
with its age inline. Everything rhymes with the console's established
character: hairlines, small caps, mono figures, cyan only for accents,
tooltips on every glyph.
