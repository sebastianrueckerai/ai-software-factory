DESIGN DIRECTION 2, REFINED — GLYPH CLUSTER, LARGER AND LIT. The chat's
window controls are one quiet cluster of icon glyphs at the right end of
the chat's head, in the grammar of a code editor's panel controls: a
diagonal-arrow expand glyph (chat across the whole screen) and a
thread glyph, each drawn 16 px LARGE on a 24-by-24 pixel target — big
enough to read as arrows, never a speck. Muted until hover; a tooltip is
visible beside the expand glyph reading "The chat across the whole
screen, the board hidden; click for the board back". When the chat IS
full screen the expand glyph swaps to its collapse twin (arrows inward)
and its square stays lit; the cluster sits alone at the screen's top
right; a faint "board" ghost of the board's first column edge shows at
the left as a promise of return, and dragging the border back past a
fifth of the screen returns it. The border between board and chat is a
plain hairline that grows a three-dot grip on hover; the chat never
narrows below 24 rem with the thread list unfolded — the threads table
gets the width it needs and the conversation column yields. The composer
has no button at all: it auto-grows with the four-line draft to about a
third of the screen, then scrolls inside. The open status panel is the
instrument's own strip language now: its cells carry the small square
lamps (state, working, waiting, need-you, spend), and the panel
compresses to two blocks — spend gauges as thin bars with mono figures at
the left; at the right the dependencies as FIVE name-plus-lamp chips in
one row (all green): forgejo, object storage, postgres, provider api,
temporal, spelled exactly, one chip's tooltip visible reading
"up · under a minute ago"; above them one mono line "model gateway up ·
2 runs waiting on the provider · GitHub sf/ai-software-factory ·
configuration 4f2c9e1". A down dependency turns red and keeps its name.
Tooltips on every glyph; nothing unlabelled; no word button anywhere.
