DESIGN DIRECTION 3 — WORDS THAT TELL THE TRUTH. Keep words, but every
control's word states what clicking it NOW does, and swaps with its state.
The chat head's button reads "Full screen" with a small expand glyph; once
the chat is full screen the same control reads "‹ Board" with an inward
glyph — the way back is a sentence, not a pressed style. The thread
switcher's control reads "Threads ‹N›" folded and "Hide" unfolded. The
composer needs no control: the box grows with the message to about a third
of the screen and scrolls inside; a quiet "shorter/longer" chevron pair at
the composer's top right collapses it back. The border between board and
chat is a hairline with a centered three-dot grip that appears on hover,
tooltip "Drag to resize — double-click to reset". The chat keeps 24 rem
minimum with the thread list unfolded; the threads table aligns its
mark/thread/last columns and never wraps a thread's name mid-word. The
open status panel: spend gauges left; right, one readout line
"model gateway up · 2 runs waiting on the provider · GitHub
sf/ai-software-factory · configuration 4f2c9e1"; under it the dependencies
as a single mono line of five lamps and names, ages on hover, a down
service red and loud. Every glyph and every non-obvious word carries a
tooltip.
