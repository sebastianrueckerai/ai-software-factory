You are a design critic for an operations console (a mission-control web
app for an autonomous software factory). What follows are two contact
sheets of the same seven design directions for the console's compact
screen state, dark theme first, light theme second. The style is settled
and pinned: every direction renders with the production stylesheet — the
same colors, hairlines, small-capitals labels, monospace tabular figures,
13 px sans words, cyan only as an accent, amber only for attention, red
only for down. The directions may only reorganize: what sits where, which
control answers which problem, what appears when.

Every direction draws the same busy content: the top bar, the board of
work cards, the agent chat docked left at a narrow split (~384 px) with
its thread list and a five-line message being typed, the status strip
with its detail panel (or its compressed form), and where a drawer is
open, a card drawer over the board.

The directions differ in how they answer five problems:
1. The split control at compact widths: how the chat is widened or made
   full screen, and how the user gets back (edge grip, head toggle, tab
   pair, or a floor under the chat).
2. Where the threads list lives: beside the conversation (the current
   console) or behind a tab that gives the conversation the full panel.
3. The composer's room: at a narrow split the message box must hold a
   long draft; judge whether each direction's composer stays usable.
4. The status panel's compression: the services as a row of lamp chips
   with detail on hover, spend gauges kept.
5. The card drawer: whether it can also hold a chat about the card
   without leaving the card's context.

For EACH direction, in its sheet cell, judge:
- Craft: is every text legible, aligned, uncut? Do the thread rows, the
  lanes, the strip line up? (Name any text you cannot read.)
- Wayfinding: could a user who has never seen it (a) widen or full-screen
  the chat, (b) get back to the board, (c) find the other threads,
  (d) type a long message without fighting the box?
- Character: does it stay inside the console's instrument look? In the
  light sheet: does the direction keep the light theme, or wash out?
- Honesty of density: does the compact state still show real work, or
  does it hide the board behind chrome?

Then:
- A numbered ranking of the seven directions, best first, one line of
  reason each.
- The two you would carry into a refinement loop, named.
- Any direction you would drop outright and why.

Do not redesign the directions; judge them.
