## 2. Claims the docs make that are NOT built (never show as real)

- Messenger push on Telegram and Discord (roadmap 145): bots exist and a live test message arrived on 2026-10-13, but "the service, the manifest and the service worker are not built". Web Push and the PWA are the same item. WhatsApp and Signal are ruled out or unofficial. The messengers are pagers only: no chat, no answer buttons (ruling 2026-10-17).
- Per-project sandbox images from a `factory/images.yaml` tool file, signing and digest pinning (docs/sandbox-images-design-2026-10-19.md): "Nothing here is built." Roadmap 281 reads "to be decided, needs research".
- Browser screenshots by an agent. The sandbox has no browser. Every UI card needs a human look before approval (escalation-ladder-redesign end of section 5). Lab gate and vision critic in the pipeline: a proposed item, not built.
- Metrics page (roadmap 147): roadmap says "discussed, not scoped". design-decisions C30 records a picked look and the concept lists a catalogue. Treat the page as unconfirmed until checked in the console. Lead time and time-to-restore need `merge_commit` and `ci_runs` data that "collects since 2026-10-20".
- Forgejo 17 and the run webhook / rerun API: held back, the makers advise against 17.0.0 (roadmap 302 Phase C).
- Warm pools, microVM server, reader/writer split, command-level sandbox policy, second factoryd replica, Harvester: rejected (roadmap-risks-and-rejections.md 107 to 114, 157).
- Mutation testing as a routine (roadmap 65), post-deploy verification by intent and recorded browser sessions (69), anti-cheat rule against deleted or skipped tests (64), intervention rate as a diff share (68), agent-to-agent question chain (30): open.
- Seed data shaped like real users' accounts: parked.
- Multi-harness choice per task class (pi, goose): the interface exists, one implementation (opencode) is wired.
- Per-project risk classes: global only.
- Enterprise SSO: Keycloak "later, not before".
- Unattended funding at full auto needs a live member session (12 h). Listed open for Sebastian in roadmap 302.
- Reproducibility claims: wipe and re-seed is a design constraint with a recorded drill (Stage 9 close lost its nodes and the database came back from continuous archive). The seed bundle holds secrets by hand and an unseal key, so re-seed is not zero-touch.

## 3. Five sentences worth stealing

1. "Reliability comes from how an AI step is built, not from its prompt." (CLAUDE.md, "Design principle for every AI step", Sebastian 2026-10-12). Companion in roadmap.md: "A prompt rule is the last resort, since such rules are brittle and accumulate."
2. "Every wait has a bound, every bound has a verdict, every verdict has a worker." (docs/chain-and-autonomy-design-2026-10-20.md section 5)
3. "A click without reading is no control, the audit and the pinned mirror are." (chain-and-autonomy 7.2)
4. "A test written after the code bends to whatever the code happens to do and proves nothing beyond itself. Named first, it is a claim made in public, at the cheapest moment to challenge it." (docs/spec-driven.md, "What the factory does that others do not")
5. "Normal states whisper. Exceptional states speak." (architecture.md 5.7, from the UX conversation). Close runners-up: "Policy lives outside the model, in code and network rules. Identity belongs to the task, not the machine. Agents exchange artifacts, and Git is the coordination mechanism." (target-architecture.md section 2) and "A made-up line dressed in the template borrows trust it did not earn." (question-format-concept).

## 4. Where the docs say we are weak, or not to advertise

- Model egress. The pitch sells "source code would leave the perimeter to a SaaS vendor" as the problem. The architecture says one provider serves all chat models today (Alibaba, target-architecture.md line 164, "because one account is simpler to fund") and the coding aliases ran on z.ai and DashScope. Code therefore reaches an outside model provider. The docs say the design does not depend on the provider and LiteLLM groups tiers. No self-hosted model path is documented. A regulated reader will ask. Do not claim "nothing leaves your perimeter" without a stated model option.
- Cost and quota. Roadmap "Next": the GLM Coding Plan quota ran out within a day and DashScope was "too dear for heavy work", so ruling 325 says heavy work runs in the local session and cards enter the factory "only to test the factory". Do not show the factory as the only builder of itself.
- Review quality on the fix loop. On card #854 seven of the review's eight findings were false (redesign section 4, problem 2). A false finding repeated three times on #836 until the human opened main. The check-in agent is the fix. The 2026-10-17 validation says only three check-in records existed on two cards, none completed, and calls its own findings "notes, not a verdict". Do not claim the ladder is proven.
- The factory is 23 days old in its rulings (first cards 2026-09-24, pitch 2026-10-13). The pitch's 128,000 lines, 1,500 commits and "three weeks" are dated 13 October. Recount before the film.
- "Fully autonomous" overreach. Forbidden and migration classes are human at every level, money beyond the pool and the month cap are the human's, and a UI card needs a human look. The roadmap's own phrasing: "fewer manual steps and gates than other products while keeping the security it needs".
- Security items the docs accept as open (roadmap-risks 88 to 106): Forgejo tokens and message bearers in plaintext in Postgres and its S3 backups. a projects token that can transfer repositories. the node agent runs as root. the sandbox can still reach Forgejo. Forgejo SSH offers only an ssh-rsa host key. a sandbox can forge OTLP records. a 33 MB binary in both remotes' history. the SigNoz webhook password files tasks. Do not film credentials screens or claim "no secret in Postgres".
- Single points: one factoryd replica (rejected to change, ruling 269), one CI runner running one job at a time, a gate supervisor that assumes one replica.
- Cut calls book their whole hold until the settle pass, so a stopped task's total errs high (ruling 115, 196, accepted risk 98).
- Hold-out claim: the doc says SWE-bench Verified "rejected correct solutions and was abandoned". It is a market-research claim inside the concept. Check the source before it goes on screen.
- Competitor names. The pitch names Devin, Factory and Conductor. Say "closed vendor services" on screen unless the survey (docs/market-research/SYNTHESIS.md, not read here) is re-verified.
- Prior sentence to avoid: "the only way to run the whole loop autonomously" (pitch B2). It is an unprovable superlative and reads as marketing.
- Dead or reversed claims. "A roll is no card" (2026-10-18) was reversed on 2026-10-20. Rulings 76, 77, 109 are reversed for the high class. The code project's merge rule was `human` until the evening of 2026-10-20. Use the dated state.
- Images of the console shown in the film must come from the lab or the live console, not from prototypes in design/prototypes. The design process doc says the real console is the baseline.
